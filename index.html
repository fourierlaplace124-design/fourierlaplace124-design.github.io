Low-Rank Clebsch–Gordan Coupling for Equivariant Spherical Forecasting

Yaojun Li, Zhixuan Zhao, Dongzhe Zheng, Christine Allen-Blanchette

LCG-SFNO uses low-rank Clebsch–Gordan tensor products to preserve SO(3) equivariance and improve weather forecasting accuracy. The SSHCG library makes these interactions computationally practical.

Yixun Hu, Zhixuan Zhao, Tao Zhong, Christine Allen-Blanchette
