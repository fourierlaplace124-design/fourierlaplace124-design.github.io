Institute of Engineering Thermophysics Tsinghua University

Sep 2025–Feb 2026

Advisor: Prof. Xianghua Xu (徐向华)

I developed a CNN surrogate to infer cell-wise correction coefficients from temperatures on two opposing surfaces, addressing non-orthogonal mesh errors in finite-volume heat-transfer simulations. I led the implementation, including ANSYS mesh parsing, and benchmarked the model against least-squares-based methods on simple 3D geometries.
